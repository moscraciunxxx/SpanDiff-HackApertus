Zero generated tokens. Development forward pass: 564,349 tokens across 1,619 windows. Judge command: make run. Reproduce the committed test and full files from public text fields with make predict-test.

How it works

Each side is encoded alone in 512-token windows. Hidden state 15 is centered (beta 0.50), compared with bidirectional CSLS (k = 5), blended with a match inside the paired sentence plus or minus one, pooled to whitespace tokens, and smoothed with a window-2 triangle. The submitted score is that frozen pipeline. Later trials did not replace it.
